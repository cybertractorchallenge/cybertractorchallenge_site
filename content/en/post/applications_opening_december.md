It's almost that time of year again where we sow the seeds to grow the future of CyberSecurity in Agriculture!

**Applications for the next CyberTractor Challenge will open at the beginning of December.** Stay tuned to [cybertractorchallenge.org](https://cybertractorchallenge.org) — when applications open, this is where you'll find the announcement and the link to apply.

Want a head start? [Last year's application post](/post/apply) is a good guide to the kind of background we're looking for:

- Currently enrolled as a full time student pursuing a 2 year, 4 year, or advanced program at an accredited college or university
- Experience with cybersecurity and/or embedded engineering technologies
- Background in networking, cloud, IoT, CANBUS, Unix-like systems, or general security concepts
- Exposure to one or more programming languages: Python, C/C++, Bash/Shell

At the event you will:

- Learn new skills and network with engineers
- Assess REAL Agriculture-focused embedded technology stacks
- Make your own tools for embedded security research
- Create lasting employer relationships
- Bolster personal and professional growth

NOTE: Thanks to our generous sponsors, if you are accepted, all student expenses to and from the event including food, housing, and travel will be covered!

Mark your calendar for the beginning of December, and please reach out using our Contact Form if you have any questions.

The CyberTractor Challenge Team
