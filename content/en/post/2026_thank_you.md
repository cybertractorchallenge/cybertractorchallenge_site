The 2026 CyberTractor Challenge is in the books! Our 5th year of the event, held June 8th-12th, 2026 on the campus of Iowa State University, brought together students, industry leaders, academic instructors, and experts to learn about the exciting field of agriculture cybersecurity. Thanks to our outstanding sponsors and supporters, this year demonstrated our growth and potential. We aimed to create a more impactful CyberTractor Challenge, and our sponsors' support made this vision a reality.

### Who Are Our Sponsors

This year's event was made possible by the generous support of the following organizations:

- [**John Deere**](https://www.deere.com/en/)
- [**CASE**](https://www.caseih.com/)
- [**Ford**](https://www.ford.com/)
- [**Temporal**](https://temporal.io/)

### How Sponsor Support Makes a Difference

This year's challenge offered comprehensive hands-on experience with real agricultural equipment:

- **Access for Everyone**: All costs covered for students and educators, including travel, housing, and food.
- **Mentorship and Collaboration**: Industry experts worked directly with students and educators, building lasting professional connections.
- **Real-World Learning**: Students assessed real agriculture-focused embedded technology, developed hypotheses, and presented their findings to the sponsoring companies.

### More Than a Sponsor

Special thanks to [**Iowa State University**](https://www.iastate.edu/) for hosting the 2026 CyberTractor Challenge. Your facilities and support were essential to our success. We also thank the Digital Ag team for contributing their time and expertise. For those interested in cybersecurity or advanced degrees in a variety of fields, ISU offers a great opportunity to grow your skills.
